# Consent Form

## Introduction

You are being asked to participate in a research study. Before you agree, however, you must be fully informed about the purpose of the study, the procedures to be followed, and any benefits, risks or discomfort that you may experience as a result of your participation. This form presents information about the study so that you may make a fully informed decision regarding your participation.

We are inviting you to participate in a research study evaluating a visualization system for analyzing rankings with respect to fairness. You will be asked to use this interactive visualization to answer several questions about a dataset while thinking aloud. **Your screen and your voice will be recorded during the study.** Afterward, you will be asked some basic demographic questions. The entire study will take approximately 30 minutes.

To participate in this study you must be 18 years or older.

## Requirements

To take part, you will need:

- A desktop or laptop computer (not a phone or tablet).
- A working microphone.
- A quiet place where you can speak aloud without being interrupted.

## Procedures to be followed

Your participation will consist of a one-time online session where you will be asked to interact with the visualization system and answer tasks. This may include some open-ended text responses about your strategy in using the system.

**Think-aloud.** Throughout the study, we will ask you to "think aloud": to say out loud what you are looking at, what you are thinking, and why you are making each decision, as you work. There are no right or wrong things to say; we are interested in your reasoning, not in evaluating you. Some tasks will ask you to confirm that you have verbalized your thoughts before continuing.

**Screen and audio recording.** At the start of the study, your browser will ask for permission to share your screen and use your microphone. Please share only the browser tab or window in which the study is running. Recording runs only while you are completing the study and stops when you finish or leave the study. We also record how you interact with the system (for example clicks, drag-and-drop actions, and the rankings you create).

Before starting, please close any windows, tabs, or notifications that might show personal information, and avoid saying your name or other identifying information aloud during the study.

## Risks to study participants

This study involves the following risks: minimal fatigue from computer use, and possible mild discomfort from speaking your thoughts aloud while being recorded. Because your voice and screen are recorded, there is a small risk that personal information could be captured if it appears on your screen or is spoken aloud; the precautions above are intended to minimize this. There may be other risks that we cannot predict. In case of discomfort, you may choose to discontinue your participation at any time without penalty.

## Benefits to research participants and others

There are no direct benefits from participating in this research. We hope that, in the future, other people using this tool may be benefited to generate and analyze fair consensus ranking.

## Record keeping and confidentiality

The study will be conducted online. We will not ask for your name. Because your voice is recorded, the recordings themselves could potentially identify you, so they are treated as confidential research data:

- Screen and audio recordings will be stored on secure, password-protected storage accessible only to the research team.
- Recordings will be transcribed for analysis. Any identifying information in the transcripts will be removed.
- Recordings will not be published or shared outside the research team. Only short, anonymized quotes from transcripts or written responses may appear in publications or presentations, without any information that could identify you.
- Recordings will be retained for [RETENTION PERIOD per approved IRB protocol] and then deleted.

Records of your participation in this study will be held confidential so far as permitted by law. However, the study investigators and, under certain circumstances, the Worcester Polytechnic Institute Institutional Review Board (WPI IRB) will be able to inspect and have access to confidential data that identify you. Any publication or presentation of the data will not identify you. In addition, to keep your information safe, the researchers will keep everything you say on a password-protected database. If you decide not to finish the study, your data, including any partial screen and audio recordings, will not be used in any analyses or publications and will be deleted.

## Compensation or treatment in the event of injury

We do not anticipate any situation in which injury could occur as a result of participation in this study. In the unlikely event of an injury, no compensation will be made for injuries resulting from participation in this study. You do not give up any of your legal rights by signing this statement.
